One-Folder Starter Kit

Your notes, your people, your to-do list and a small AI team, all in one folder.
Tus notas, tu gente, tus pendientes y un pequeño equipo de IA, todo en una carpeta.

Download the kit / Descargar Keep your files safe (guide) / Protege tus archivos

A zip file, about 130 KB. Mac only for now. / Un archivo zip, unos 130 KB. Por ahora solo Mac.

Setup

  1. Download the zip and double-click it.
  2. Move the starter-kit folder into your Dropbox and rename it to your name plus OS, like LeOS or JandrOS.
  3. Open README-START-HERE.md and follow the numbered steps.
  4. If your Mac won't open a .command file, right-click it and choose Open. You only do this once.

Instalación

  1. Descarga el zip y haz doble clic.
  2. Mueve la carpeta starter-kit a tu Dropbox y ponle tu nombre más OS, como LeOS o JandrOS.
  3. Abre README-START-HERE.md y sigue los pasos numerados.
  4. Si tu Mac no abre un archivo .command, haz clic derecho y elige Abrir. Solo la primera vez.

Install your AI / Instala tu IA

Open Terminal (Command + Space, type Terminal), paste one line, press Return. No Homebrew needed. / Abre Terminal, pega una línea y pulsa Return. No necesitas Homebrew.

Claude Code (Claude Pro or Max):
curl -fsSL https://claude.ai/install.sh | bash

Codex (ChatGPT Plus or higher):
curl -fsSL https://chatgpt.com/codex/install.sh | sh

Or double-click 02-install-claude-code.command or 02-install-codex.command in the kit. / O haz doble clic en el archivo 02 que corresponda.

What's inside / Qué hay dentro

  1. team-inbox/: Team Inbox. You drop things in; voice memos land in team-inbox/voice/.
  2. owners-inbox/_New/: Owner's Inbox. Finished work waiting for your review.
  3. team/: your AI teammates, including an orchestrator and a senior researcher.
  4. SOPs/: your rules, so the system knows your quality standard.
  5. knowledge/: your finished, approved work, one folder per course.
  6. past-work/: work that missed the mark, plus failure-journal.md. Every miss becomes a rule.
  7. CLAUDE.md: the brain. Codex reads AGENTS.md, which points to the same file.
  8. journal.md: the work log. End every session with /close-chat (Codex: $close-chat).

Hard tasks to try / Retos difíciles

Each one teaches the system something. When it gets one wrong, write the fix in past-work/failure-journal.md and turn it into a rule. / Cada error se convierte en una regla.

  1. Week plan: "Read my course folder and build next week's lesson plan, one page per class, in my voice." Then mark what sounds wrong and ask for a rule that fixes it.
  2. Ten memos, one day: record ten voice memos (mix English and Spanish), then ask, "What did I promise people today? Who did I mention that has no profile?"
  3. A PDF that passes: ask for a one-page handout with a table. Check it against your PDF SOP. Make it fix every miss without you saying how.
  4. Hire a teammate: "Create a new teammate for X (grading rubrics, Spanish grammar, a hobby). Write their profile and one SOP." Use them for a week.
  5. Your own command: build /plan-tomorrow that reads todo.md and your calendar notes and gives you three things to do first.
  6. Research with sources: "Find three studies on X. Every claim gets a link. Say what you couldn't confirm." Then check one link yourself.
  7. Clean-up day: "Find duplicate people, broken links and stale to-dos in my vault. Propose fixes; don't change anything until I say yes."
  8. Teach it back: "Quiz me on how my own system works." If you can't answer, neither can a new teammate. Fix the docs.
  9. Safety test: try the fake-secret check from the safety guide. Then ask it to explain every rule in your settings file in plain words.
  10. Boss level: a small web page for your class (an agenda board or a vocab game) that opens by double-click. Ask Claude or Codex to explain every part.
